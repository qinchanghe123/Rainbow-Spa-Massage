Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers before your visit.

How early should I arrive?

Please arrive about 5–10 minutes early so you have time to check in and relax before your session.

Can I get a massage if I have an injury?

If you have an injury, recent surgery, or medical condition, please consult your doctor first and let us know before your session begins.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please call as soon as possible if you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment.

Is there parking available?

Parking is available near the location for customer convenience.
